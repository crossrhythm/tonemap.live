2. Free Tier

Tonemap's core features are available free of charge with no account required. The free tier includes real-time pitch detection, color-grid visualization, Note View, and basic tuning options.

3. Tonemap Pro Subscription

Tonemap Pro is an optional paid subscription available at $9.99 USD per year, providing access to extended features. A Pro subscription is how Tonemap stays independent: no advertising, no outside funding, no selling of user data. If you find the tool useful, Pro is the way to support it.

Subscriptions are billed annually and renew automatically unless cancelled. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period — you retain Pro access until then.

On the web (tonemap.live), payments are processed securely by Polar, and you may cancel at any time through your account portal or by contacting us. Tonemap does not store your payment information.

In the iOS app, the subscription is purchased through the App Store and billed to your Apple Account. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and can be managed or cancelled at any time in your device's App Store subscription settings. Apple handles the transaction; Tonemap never receives your payment details.

The two subscriptions are independent: an App Store subscription unlocks Pro in the iOS app, and a web subscription unlocks Pro at tonemap.live.

6. Privacy & Data

Tonemap processes audio input locally on your device — in your browser using the Web Audio API on the web, and entirely on-device using Apple's audio frameworks in the iOS app. Audio and usage data is never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Basic, anonymized page-view statistics may be collected on the tonemap.live website to understand how the tool is being used. The iOS app contains no analytics or tracking frameworks and collects no data. No personally identifiable information is collected from free-tier users, and no data is sold or shared with third parties. Web Pro subscribers provide an email address and payment information solely for billing purposes; this is handled by Polar in accordance with their privacy policy. App Store subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple; see Apple's privacy policy.

Authentication cookie (web app). When you activate a Tonemap Pro license on tonemap.live, a strictly necessary cookie named tm_pro is set in your browser. This cookie contains a cryptographically signed token that proves your device has an active Pro license — it does not contain your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information. The cookie is required to serve Pro content; the server cannot verify your device's authorization without it. Because it is strictly necessary to deliver the service you explicitly requested, it does not require a separate consent prompt under GDPR, the ePrivacy Directive, or comparable regulations. Your license may be activated on up to five devices simultaneously; you can deactivate a device at any time from within the web app to free a slot. License keys apply to the web app; the iOS app uses App Store subscriptions and sets no such cookie. The cookie persists for up to one year and is refreshed automatically when your license is revalidated. Tonemap's server infrastructure runs on Cloudflare.
