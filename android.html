About the Android app

Tonemap for Android brings the same real-time pitch and intonation visualization from tonemap.live to a native Android app, built in Kotlin with no web view and no third-party frameworks. Pitch detection, rendering, and audio handling all happen on your device.

The app will be available on Google Play as Tonemap Tuner, published by East End Violin Studio. This page explains how Tonemap for Android handles your data.

Privacy Policy

No accounts, no ads, no data collection

Tonemap for Android has no sign-up and no account. It contains no advertising SDKs and no analytics or tracking frameworks, and it shows no ads. It collects no personal data, sells no data, and shares no data with third parties. The only permission it asks for is the microphone.

Microphone access

The app asks for microphone permission the first time it's opened, to power real-time pitch detection. Audio is analyzed on your device with Android's audio APIs and is never transmitted. Tonemap listens only while it is open on your screen; it stops using the microphone as soon as you leave it. If permission is denied, the tuner cannot work, but you can grant access at any time in your device's Settings.

You can choose a different microphone, such as a USB microphone or a headset, from the microphone menu in the app. Your choice and each microphone's gain setting are stored on your device only.

Quick Record

Quick Record (part of Tonemap Pro) saves the take you record as an audio file in the app's private storage on your device, so you can play it back. Each new take replaces the previous one, and the file is also removed if you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. A take leaves your device only if you choose Share, and then only to the app or person you pick.

Tonemap Pro & subscriptions

Tonemap Pro is unlocked in the app by subscribing through Google Play. Google handles the entire transaction — Tonemap's servers are not involved and never receive your name, email, or payment details. Your purchase is recorded by Google Play and tied to your Google Account; see Google's privacy policy for how Google handles that information.

Note: Tonemap Pro on Android, on iOS, and on tonemap.live are separate subscriptions — a Google Play subscription unlocks Pro in the Android app. See the Help & FAQ page for details.

Third-party services

Tonemap's Android app relies on one outside service: Google Play, for purchases, and only if you choose to subscribe. The app itself embeds no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or other third-party frameworks.

Data retention & deletion

Tonemap's servers retain no data about your use of the Android app. Your settings and any Quick Record take are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app. Subscription records are held by Google Play. Data handling for the tonemap.live website is described in the web Terms & Privacy section. Questions about any record we hold can be sent to support@tonemap.live.

Children's privacy

Tonemap is made for musicians of all ages, including young students. The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13: it has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics, and audio never leaves the device unless a user chooses to share a recording. The app follows Google Play's Families policy and applicable laws, including the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Android app evolves. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.
